# Casper (draft, pictures only)

The user's white Aspin (Filipino street dog). Generated by `tools/casper.py`.

- **Look:** lean, sturdy build with a deep chest and a tucked waist. Short white coat (`#f7f3ea`, shade `#e2d9c8`), a wedge-shaped head with a long snout, semi-floppy ears with cream-tan tips (`#ecd1a6`).
- **Face:** amber eyes (`#c98a34`) with pink rims, a pink nose (`#c98686`), a happy panting mouth with a pink tongue, and brown toenails.
- **Personality:** he **barks when you interact with him** ("!" pop, then "Arf! Arf!") and does a **backflip**: crouch, launch, curl into a ball and spin backwards in an arc, land, then a happy bark.
- **`casper_sheet.png` layout** (38×30 cells):
  - rows 1–4: walk right / left / down / up (4 frames each)
  - row 5: sit, sit wag, bark A, bark B
  - row 6: stand, crouch, tuck (the flip ball, rotated in code), sit blink
- **`casper_demo.gif`:** he walks in, sits, you interact, he barks, backflips, lands, barks again and sits.
