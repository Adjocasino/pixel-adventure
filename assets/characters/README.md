- Rows (top to bottom): **down, right, up, left**.
- Columns: **walk 0–3**, then **idle 0** (stand), **idle 1** (breath), **idle 2** (blink).
- Style: **no bangs** (soft middle part showing the forehead), straight hair to just above the chest.
- Hair: rose-brown with a touch more red (based on the user's reference photo): `#7a4338` (shade `#602e26`, dark `#3c1a15`, highlight `#9a5a4c`, sheen `#c48676`). Blush `#e8565c`.

## Inad broom ride (`inad_broom_sheet.png`, previews `inad_broom_ride.png` / `.gif`)
- Run animation: hold B and she flies on a broom. 4 frames of **28×33 px** (origin offset 6,7) per direction. Sheet rows: **down, up, right** (left = mirrored right). Down: handle points at the viewer, bristles peek out at both sides. Up: big bristle fan toward the viewer. Side: side-saddle. Preview: `inad_broom_all_dirs.png`/`.gif`.
- Hover bob, hair blown back, fluttering straw bristles with a soft-blue tie, boots dangling. In game: hovers ~4px above her shadow, 2.3 px/frame, sparkle trail, soft whoosh.
- Generated by `makeGirl('side', f, {ride:1})`.

## Jellyfish emote (`jellyfish_emote.png`)
- Two 16×18 jellyfish frames (bell wide / bell squeezed).
- **Two-jellyfish dodge emote:** two jellyfish pop out beside the character's head. The character hops and reaches for the right one, which zips away (afterimage + sparkle), then the left one, which dodges too. They drift back to tease, swirl around each other above the hat, and float off while fading (~1.8 s in game).
- `inad_jellyfish_dodge.png`/`.gif`: Inad's version with her reach poses (`makeGirl(...,{reach:'r'|'l', look:1})`).
- In the game: the small oval button above A (or the E key). The playable kid hops instead of reaching.
