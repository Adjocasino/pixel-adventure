# Bins' lines (draft)

Bins runs the animal shop. Every line invites you to eat at his place: breakfast, lunch or dinner. Turon (his dog) sits next to him.

1. Have you eaten? No? Breakfast at my place. It's always ready.
2. Tomorrow, breakfast at my house, 6 AM. Turon will save you a seat.
3. Lunch at my place later? I cooked too much again. On purpose.
4. You look hungry. You always look hungry. Dinner, my place, 7 o'clock.
5. Breakfast is the most important meal of the day. That's why I invite you every day.
6. My door is open for breakfast, lunch and dinner. Merienda too, if you knock twice.
7. You skipped breakfast? Okay. Then come over for breakfast at lunch time.
8. Turon already ate. Now it's your turn. Come over!
