Fruit Catch: physics demo (single player)

Adjo runs with a bilao held up like a tray. Fruit really lands in it, piles up, rolls and bounces, and sudden moves slosh it out. Points only count when you empty the bilao into a crate (run to either edge). Carry a big load for a big multiplier, or bank early to play safe. Phone: drag left/right. Keyboard: ← → or A / D, P to pause. 2-minute round, 3 hearts.

Scoring

  • Banking: run to a crate at either edge and the bilao tips everything in. Fruit only scores when it lands in a crate.
  • Load multiplier: each extra fruit in one dump adds ×0.15 (5 fruit = ×1.6, 10 = ×2.35, 14+ = ×3). A golden mango in the load doubles the whole dump.
  • Weight: the more you carry, the slower Adjo runs and turns (down to half speed). Watch the weight bar.
  • Balance: real gravity: fruit only gets in over the rim, rolls and settles by friction, and can slosh out on hard turns. Fruit hitting the outside of the bilao bounces off. A heavier bilao makes Adjo slower.
  • Spilled or missed fruit just bruises on the ground: no points, no penalty.
  • Chaos ramps up: faster and more stuff falls, wind gusts start after 40 seconds, and the last 15 seconds are a fruit storm.
  • Titles: Sprout → Picker (400) → Farmer (1200) → Orchard Keeper (2500) → Mango Master (4000).
