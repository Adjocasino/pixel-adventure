Cottage Meadow: song demos, all versions

Three original songs, every version in one place. Tap a version to play it and tap again to stop. Starting one version stops the others, so you can switch and compare. Every sound is generated live in your browser from code; there are no audio files.

Willow Village song: piano version

Same melody, bass and chords as the village song in the game, played three ways. Tap one to play; tap again to stop. It loops. Use the key buttons to move the piano lower (the current game version always stays the same).

1. Current (in the game now)
Soft triangle melody, sine bass and a quiet square arpeggio.
2. Solo piano
Right hand plays the melody, left hand plays the bass and broken chords, with a little room echo. A touch slower and gentler.
3. Piano + soft strings
The same piano, with a warm string pad holding the chords underneath. Cozier, more "morning in the village".

"Quiet Field": a new original song

Same key and tempo as the reference (E minor, about 48 BPM, slow), with a brand-new melody and chords. 16 bars, about 80 seconds, loops. Tap to play, tap again to stop.

1. Solo piano
Sparse right-hand melody over slow rolling left-hand chords, with room echo.
2. Piano + soft pad
The same piano with a quiet, warm pad underneath, for a dreamier, wide-open feel.
3. Piano + low harp
The piano keeps the melody and the low root note. A deep, warm harp rolls through the chords underneath, with a sweep at the end of each section.
4. Piano + mid harp
The same, with the harp an octave higher: brighter and clearer, sitting between the piano's bass and melody.

"Porch Light": a new original song

Sitting on the porch with your wife, looking out over the meadow, just enjoying life. A warm major key with a slow, rocking-chair sway (6/8). Loops. Tap to play, tap again to stop.

1. Solo piano
A simple, hummable melody over gently rocking chords.
2. Porch version
The piano with a soft warm pad and birds in the meadow. From the second time around, a soft flute joins in harmony, like a second person humming along.
3. Piano + harp
Just two instruments: the piano plays the melody and a low bass note, and a low, warm harp rolls up through the chords, with a little harp sweep leading into each new section.
