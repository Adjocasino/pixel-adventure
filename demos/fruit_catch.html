Fruit Catch: demo (single player)

Adjo balances a woven bilao on his hat under the mango tree. Catch the good fruit, dodge the bad stuff. Phone: drag left/right anywhere on the game. Keyboard: ← → or A / D, P to pause. You have 3 hearts.

Scoring

  • Combo: every good catch in a row adds to the combo. 10 in a row = ×1.5, 20 = ×2, 30 = ×2.5, 40+ = ×3.
  • Letting a good fruit hit the ground breaks the combo, but costs no points.
  • It gets faster over time, and more bad things fall.
  • Every bad thing has a warning: flies, a buzz, a shadow on the ground, or a bird calling first.
  • Game over at 0 hearts. Titles: Sprout → Picker (500) → Farmer (1500) → Orchard Keeper (3000) → Mango Master (5000).
